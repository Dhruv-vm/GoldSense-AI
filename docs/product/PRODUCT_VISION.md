# Product Vision — GoldSense AI

## 1. Executive Summary
Precious metals in India represent both a cultural foundation and a major store of private wealth. However, the domestic spot pricing of gold and silver in India diverges meaningfully from international futures benchmarks (such as COMEX Gold `GC=F` or LBMA spot) due to complex import tariffs (Basic Customs Duty and AIDC), currency fluctuations (`USDINR=X`), and localized physical supply and demand dynamics in key bullion hubs like Ahmedabad (represented by KJPL - Kundanmal Jewellers Private Limited).

**GoldSense AI** was created to demystify this pricing gap, empower personal bullion investors to monitor physical holdings with zero-leakage financial intelligence, and explore machine-learning-driven forward price projections.

---

## 2. Target Audience & Core Persona
1. **Personal Bullion Investor / Collector (Primary)**:
   - Holds physical 24K bars, 22K jewelry, or 999 silver coins.
   - Needs accurate mark-to-market valuation based on real physical spot rates (not paper COMEX contracts).
   - Wants to track unrealized gains, tax basis/acquisition cost, and fine weight purity.
2. **Quantitative Commodity Analyst (Secondary)**:
   - Seeks to model the domestic premium spread between global USD gold and Indian spot INR gold.
   - Requires rigorous, zero-lookahead financial news sentiment metrics alongside price momentum.

---

## 3. Core Product Principles
- **No Commercial Selling Workflows**: Explicitly non-commercial. No merchant point-of-sale (POS), no invoices, no customer checkout, no supplier ledgers.
- **Data Honesty Above All**: Clearly communicate data limitations, model sample sizes, and freshness states. Never fabricate backtests or predictions.
- **Physical Market Alignment**: Ground all valuation and predictions in actual Indian delivery rates (INR per gram) rather than abstract dollar-denominated futures.
